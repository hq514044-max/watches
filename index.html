Our Story

The Maison

A Legacy of Refinement

Founded in Geneva in 1987, Aurum began as a single atelier dedicated to the art of fine watchmaking. Today, we house three distinct collections — timepieces, fragrances, and studs — each crafted with the same uncompromising standards.

Our perfumers source rare ingredients from Grasse, our gemologists hand-select every stone, and our watchmakers assemble each movement by hand. The result: objects of desire built to last generations.
